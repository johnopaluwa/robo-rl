# research-notes/

Your own notes: papers read, courses taken, experiments that failed and why,
conversations with business owners. Keep a running [`log.md`](log.md) — it's
boring to write and invaluable later (for your own learning, and as raw
material for a build-in-public log, a pitch deck, or onboarding a first hire).

For each Phase 0 customer-discovery conversation, duplicate
[`customer-discovery-template.md`](customer-discovery-template.md). Record
only what was actually said or observed; don't fill gaps with assumptions.
