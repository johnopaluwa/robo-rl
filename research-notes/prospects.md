# Prospect list — Phase 0 customer conversations (bakery tray task)

Started 2026-10-06. This file lives OUTSIDE `interviews/` on purpose:
`verify.sh` counts only real conversation notes in `research-notes/interviews/`,
and a prospect list is not evidence.

**Goal:** 5 conversations with bakery owners / production leads / facility
managers. Not selling — validating. Use the zero-risk pitch script in
[docs/BUSINESS_PLAN.md](../docs/BUSINESS_PLAN.md) §2 and record each
conversation with the
[customer-discovery template](customer-discovery-template.md).

## Who to approach (in order of expected accessibility)

1. **Small/mid craft bakeries with central production + delivery rounds**
   around Magdeburg (Saxony-Anhalt). They load/unload trays and rack trolleys
   multiple times a day, pre-dawn shifts are hardest to staff, and they are
   too small for Chef Robotics / ROBOMATIC-class systems.
   - Find via: Google Maps / Gelbe Seiten for "Bäckerei Produktion Magdeburg",
     "Großbäckerei Sachsen-Anhalt", Innung des Bäckerhandwerks (bakers' guild)
     member lists for the Magdeburg region.
2. **Co-packers and small food assembly lines** (sandwich, pastry, snack
   packing) in the Magdeburg–Halle–Leipzig corridor — tray loading is a
   named fit in BUSINESS_PLAN.md §1.
3. **Frozen-dough / bake-off suppliers** (depalletize → tray → oven is a
   documented robot use case, see Malzers case study) — more industrial, keep
   as backup if craft bakeries show no pain.

## Conversation checklist (keep consistent across all 5)

- [ ] Observe or get described the actual tray/trolley flow, and its volume
      (trays/hour, shifts/day).
- [ ] Ask about hiring: which shift/task is hardest to staff, and what
      turnover looks like.
- [ ] Ask what breaks today when the task is slow or done wrong (missed
      delivery rounds, oven idle time, burnt/broken product).
- [ ] Ask what "good enough" would be — never propose a number first.
- [ ] Ask constraints: tray/trolley formats, floor space, food-hygiene rules,
      who approves spending.
- [ ] Record pilot conditions and budget owner per the template.
